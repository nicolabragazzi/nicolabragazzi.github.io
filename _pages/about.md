Nicola Luigi Bragazzi is a medical doctor, epidemiologist, mathematical modeller and data scientist. He is Research Group Leader of Mathematical and Computational Modelling for Epidemic Response (MCMER) at {{ site.author.employer }}, in Kigali, Rwanda.

His work combines epidemiology, biostatistics, mathematical modelling and artificial intelligence to investigate infectious diseases, vaccination and population health. His research also encompasses evidence synthesis, immunology, genetics, multi-omics, and Digital and One Health.

For education and selected research appointments, see the [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}). Publications are available through [Google Scholar]({{ site.author.googlescholar }}).
