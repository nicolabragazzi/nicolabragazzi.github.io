Nicola Luigi Bragazzi, MD, PhD
======
Medical doctor, epidemiologist, mathematical modeller and data scientist.

Current appointment
------
**Research Group Leader, Mathematical and Computational Modelling for Epidemic Response (MCMER)**

AIMS Research and Innovation Centre (AIMS RIC), Kigali, Rwanda.

Education
------
* **Specialization in Public Health, Epidemiology and Biostatistics**, University of Genoa, Italy — June 2017.
* **PhD in Biophysics**, University of Marburg, Germany — January 2014.
* **Degree in Medicine**, University of Genoa, Italy — June 2011.

Selected research experience
------
* **Postdoctoral researcher, Clinical Pharmacy**, Saarland University, Saarbrücken, Germany — from August 2024; a previous appointment.
* **Postdoctoral researcher, Department of Food and Drug Sciences**, University of Parma, Italy — March 2023–July 2024.
* **Postdoctoral researcher, Laboratory for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (LIAM), Department of Mathematics and Statistics**, York University, Toronto, Canada — August 2019–February 2023.
* **Postdoctoral researcher, Public Health, Occupational and Professional Epidemiology and Biostatistics**, University of Genoa, Italy — December 2017–July 2019.

Research interests
------
Infectious disease and vaccination modelling; epidemiology and biostatistics; systematic reviews and meta-analysis; artificial intelligence in health; immunology, genetics and multi-omics; Digital and One Health.
